## V3: bounded depth pairing

### Built

- `depth.py`, new. `RgbdBuffer` holds the newest colour, the newest depth and one finished bundle, and has the mailbox interface of `NewestFrame`. An exact stamp wins, the gap is at most 5 ms and an image waits at most 20 ms of wall time on an injected monotonic clock.
- `depth.py`: `depth_to_metres()` reads 32FC1 in metres and 16UC1 in millimetres, honouring `step` and `is_bigendian`. `read_registered_depth()` gates on frames, size, intrinsics, zero distortion and rays. It reads no TF. It refuses with a reason.
- `image.py`: `calibration_problem()`. `camera_info_problem()` is unchanged.
- `node.py`: `geometry_enabled`, `depth_topic`, `depth_camera_info_topic`, `max_pair_gap_s`, `pair_wait_s`, `require_depth`. With geometry off, the mailbox stays `NewestFrame` and no depth subscription exists. The counters `depth`, `paired` and `no_depth` appear in the latency line only with geometry on.
- `config/vision.yaml`: the new block, off by default. `sim.yaml` needs no override, because the sim publishes the same topic names.
- `tests/test_depth.py`, new. `tests/test_node.py` gained pairing tests over real local rclpy peers. They cover reversed arrival, 0/4/6 ms gaps, a paused and a reset sim clock, replacement, padded rows, big-endian values, wrong encoding, frame and calibration, missing and invalid depth, and no reuse of a stale pair.
- Docs: ADR-0014 embedded on `vision.md`. ADR-0002 is "Accepted, partly superseded by ADR-0014", on its status line and block title only. `vision.md`: threads, QoS, parameters, latency counts and a new Depth pairing section. Troubleshooting rows in `docs/guides/vision-node.md`. Layout rows in `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md`.

## Decisions, with what each costs

### V3

- `geometry_enabled` is the only flag added now. Costs nothing later: V5 to V7 add their own flags.
- `geometry_enabled` gates depth, masks and quality together. You cannot get quality without the depth subscription.
- `require_depth` without geometry is refused at startup. A config that sets it alone will not start.
- A refused pair is handled like an unpaired image. A misconfigured camera under `require_depth` publishes no arrays, with only a throttled warning.
- Zero distortion is required. A camera that publishes a non-zero D for rectified images would be refused. Not checked on the ZED.
- Rays may differ by at most 1 px. A smaller error passes silently.
- No intrinsics are synthesised. A lower-resolution raster needs its own `camera_info`.
- Zero stamps never pair. A publisher with unset stamps gets no points.
- An exact stamp is preferred, so an image can wait the full 20 ms on an inexact stream. That adds up to 20 ms per image.
- A depth older than `pair_wait_s` of wall time is dropped when an image arrives. This rejects skew above 20 ms. The robot's real skew is not measured.
- `pair_wait_s` must be over 0 and at most 0.1, and the gap from 0 to 0.1. A longer wait needs a code change.
- A colour past its deadline does not pair with a late depth, even if the worker has not taken it yet. A few pairs are lost at the edge of the wait.
- A replacing colour inherits the waiting colour's deadline, so colour faster than 50 Hz cannot starve the worker. A later colour can then wait less than 20 ms.
- ADR-0014 states the counter and the one-pending-job service rules ahead of V5 and V6, so those slices do not rewrite it. If they find otherwise, they need a new ADR.

### V4

- No new latency stages until V8, which owns the per-stage logs. Geometry time is hidden: depth read in `convert`, mask copy and resize in `objects`, lift and quality in `build`. `LatencyLog.STAGES` stays pinned.
- Half-even rounding for the erosion radius (`round(2.5)` is 2). This is Python's `round`, and the brief does not say which. A different rule changes the radius on exact halves only.
- Every tracked row's mask is resized to native size, including rows the score floor drops later. This keeps alignment simple at the cost of about 2.7 MB of bool per row at HD2K, plus its resize time. Unmeasured. Resizing only kept rows is a small later change.
- The Laplacian runs only when at least one kept detection has a mask, so empty frames cost nothing. The quality function takes any mask, so V6 can pass its rectangle.
- A mask of the wrong size from a provider raises and fails the image, like any other wrong provider shape. A provider bug shows as failed frames, not as bad points.
- `r.masks` being None with `keep_masks=True` gives detections without masks, not an error. If a seg model were swapped for a non-seg one, points would stay empty with no warning from this code.
- `depth_min_m` and `depth_max_m` are node parameters, kept in `VisionConfig` with the rest. A per-profile value goes through `sim.yaml` or another file. Both limits are inclusive.
- `to_point_cloud()` applies its cap again after the lift. That is a no-op, kept so the wire rule holds for every producer.
- `box_mask()` in `fakes.py` is used only by tests. It sits there so three test files can share it.
- ADR-0013 got a "Runs today" line, because its text says later changes mark which parts run. Its older "fills none of the new data yet" consequence stays as written. A reader has to take the line above it as current.
- No new ADR for V4. The row asks for none, and the formulas are the brief's.

## Unverified

- No robot, real model, GPU or camera run. Every result here uses fakes, stand-ins and local rclpy peers.
- Real YOLOE mask alignment. The tests use a real `Results` object and pin the padding rule against the installed `scale_masks`, but no model produced the masks.
- The real `process_mask` output size at the default input size on HD2K images.
- The robot's depth and colour stamp gap, its depth raster size, and whether its `camera_info` has zero distortion.
- `depth/camera_info` on the robot, if its depth is downsampled.
- CPU and memory costs of the raster copy, the mask copy and resize, and the lift at HD2K, and their effect on the 5 Hz and 300 ms targets.
- MuJoCo's rendered depth through the whole chain. Its config says colour and depth come from one camera, read from `walkie-ros` `main`.
- The depth range and the quality formula are uncalibrated.
- `c12` was not run or extended. That is V8's.
